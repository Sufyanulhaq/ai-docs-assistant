# Refunds and cancellation

## Refund window

Every first purchase of a paid plan has a 14 day money back guarantee. If you are not happy, ask for a refund within 14 days of your first payment and you will get all of it back. Email billing@fernhill.example from the address on the account.

## After the first 14 days

- Monthly plans: payments are not refundable once the 14 days have passed, but you can cancel at any time and you will not be charged again.
- Annual plans: there are no refunds after the first 14 days. Cancelling stops automatic renewal, and the plan stays active until the end of the paid year.

Extra runs charged as overage are not refundable, unless the charge was caused by a fault on the Fernhill side.

## How to cancel

Only an Owner can cancel. Go to Settings, then Billing, then Cancel plan, and confirm. Your workspace keeps its paid features until the end of the period you already paid for, then moves to the Free plan.

## What happens to your data after cancelling

Your pipelines, runs and settings are kept. When the workspace moves to the Free plan, a few limits change:

- Only 3 seats keep edit access. Everyone else becomes a Viewer for 30 days, then loses access unless you upgrade again.
- Run history older than 7 days stops being available.
- API requests are limited to 60 per minute.

You can upgrade again at any time and nothing needs to be rebuilt.
