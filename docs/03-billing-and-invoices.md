# Billing and invoices

## Payment methods

Fernhill accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Business workspaces on an annual contract can also pay by ACH bank transfer in the US or SEPA direct debit in the EU. Prices can be billed in USD, EUR or GBP, and the currency is chosen when you first subscribe.

To change your card, go to Settings, then Billing, then Payment method.

## When you are charged

You are charged on the day you subscribe and again on the same day of each following month, or each following year on an annual plan. This date is your renewal date.

## Invoices

An invoice is created on every renewal date and emailed to the billing contact. You can download every past invoice as a PDF from Settings, then Billing, then Invoices. To send invoices to a different address, change the billing contact in the same place.

## Tax and VAT

Add your company details and your VAT or GST number under Settings, then Billing, then Billing details. Valid EU VAT numbers are treated as reverse charge and no VAT is added. The details only apply to future invoices, so contact support if an earlier invoice needs to be reissued.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, Fernhill retries the card on day 1, day 3, day 5 and day 7 after the failure, and emails the billing contact each time.

- If the payment is still failing after 7 days, the workspace becomes read only. Existing data stays safe and you can still view runs.
- After 30 days with no successful payment, scheduled pipelines are paused.

Updating the payment method triggers an immediate retry, and the workspace returns to normal as soon as it succeeds.
