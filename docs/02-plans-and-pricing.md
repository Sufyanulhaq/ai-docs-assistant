# Plans and pricing

Fernhill has three plans. All prices are in US dollars and shown per seat per month. A seat is any person with an Owner, Admin or Member role. Viewers are free on every plan.

## Free

- Price: $0
- Up to 3 seats
- 1,000 runs per month
- 7 days of run history
- 2 concurrent runs
- 60 API requests per minute
- Community support only

When a Free workspace reaches its monthly run limit, new runs are queued until the next billing month or until you upgrade.

## Pro

- Price: $29 per seat per month when billed monthly, or $24 per seat per month when billed annually (about 17 percent less)
- 50,000 runs per month
- 90 days of run history
- 10 concurrent runs
- 600 API requests per minute
- Email support with a reply within 1 business day
- Two factor authentication enforcement for the whole workspace

## Business

- Price: $79 per seat per month, billed annually only
- 500,000 runs per month
- 1 year of run history
- 50 concurrent runs
- 3,000 API requests per minute
- Single sign on with SAML and SCIM user provisioning
- Audit logs kept for 1 year
- Priority support with a reply within 4 hours

## Extra runs

Pro and Business workspaces are never blocked at their run limit. Extra runs are billed at $0.40 per 1,000 runs at the end of the billing period.

## Adding and removing seats

Seats you add part way through a billing period are prorated for the days that remain. If you remove a seat, the credit is applied to your next invoice.
